Indexxero · Retention readiness check

Can your export support a retention number?

Drop in the subscription export you would hand an investor or buyer. This page checks which retention figures the file can support, and says in plain words why when it cannot. It does not calculate any retention percentage.

Your file never leaves your browser. This page has no server and cannot send data anywhere.

The page carries a security policy (connect-src 'none') that tells your browser to refuse every network request it tries to make, so there is nowhere for your file to go. If you would rather see it than take our word for it, disconnect from the internet now that the page has loaded. It still works.

1. Choose a file

A CSV export from your billing, subscription or CRM system. One row per account, or one row per account per month. Commas or semicolons both work.

The sample is invented data: a monthly subscription book of 100 made up accounts (SYN-M001 to SYN-M100) over 24 months, generated from a fixed seed. No real company is in it. Some accounts have their plan left blank on purpose, so you can see how the check reports a gap.

What a pilot adds

This check tells you whether the file can support each figure. A pilot does the rest, on the same export:

  • The number, rebuilt from your own export the way an investor or buyer will rebuild it.
  • A manifest anyone can check on their own machine, so a stranger can confirm the report matches the file it came from.
  • The full list of what your data could not support, each item with its reason.

The first few pilots are free. We are a two person company in Dallas with no customers yet, and we have not yet run a real company's data. What we ask for in return is the story of how it went.
