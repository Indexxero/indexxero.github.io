Know what your retention number will look like after someone else rebuilds it.

When a company raises money or sells, the investor or buyer does not take the numbers on trust. They rebuild them from the raw data, and the price moves on what they find. Indexxero does that rebuild first, for the company, so the founder walks in already knowing what will hold.

What we do

You send us your exports. You get three things back.

The numberRevenue and retention rebuilt from your raw exports, not read off a dashboard.
A manifestA short file that lets anyone check the result on their own machine, without trusting us or connecting to anything.
What could not be measuredIf a figure cannot be supported by the data you sent, we say so in plain words instead of printing a zero.

That third one is the part other tools leave out, and it is the part a diligence team will find anyway.

What it looks like

Every line says whether it was measured.

# example, not a real company gross_revenue_retention 0.861 measured net_revenue_retention 0.974 measured logo_churn_12m 0.139 measured expansion_by_cohort nil unmeasurable: no plan history in export content_hash sha256:9f3a2c…c41e method_version 1.0.0

The last two lines are how a stranger checks that the report matches the file it came from. The fourth line is what most tools would have shown as 0.

When it matters

A few months before an investor or a buyer rebuilds your numbers.

  • Three to six months before a raise.
  • Before a sale or an acquisition.
  • When a private equity buyer or a lender asks for the numbers.
  • Your first outside audit.
  • The board asks for cohort retention and nobody is sure the figure is right.

Outside those moments this is not a problem anyone feels. That is fine. It is built for the moment, not for every day.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. You send the exports you already have. CSV or spreadsheet exports from your billing, subscription or CRM system.
  2. We rebuild the number and produce the manifest. We tell you how long that will take before you send anything, and if your columns need mapping we say so up front rather than guessing.
  3. You get the result, the manifest and the list of what could not be measured, and we walk you through it.

Before you send anything, we tell you in writing where your file will live, who can see it, and when it is deleted.
